Safar Planner & Travel Tips

Smart preparation for a smooth, stress-free journey across every destination

Essential Travel Guide

Category Key Tips Quick Reminder
Packing Pack versatile layers for Skardu's chilly nights, comfortable walking shoes for Rome, and a universal travel adapter. Roll clothes to save suitcase space and keep essentials in your carry-on.
Budgeting Set a daily spending limit for food and tickets, carry small local cash notes, and book flights early. Keep an emergency reserve separate from your daily wallet.
Documents Keep your passport, visas, travel insurance, and hotel confirmations organized in a waterproof folder. Save digital scans of all IDs on your phone and email before departure.
